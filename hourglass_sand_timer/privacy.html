Hourglass — Privacy Policy

Hosted at: https://alphaloop-studio.github.io/policies/hourglass_sand_timer/privacy.html

Effective: 2026-10-14 (app version 1.1)

Hourglass is a visual sand timer made by Alpha Loop. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

1. No account

There is no sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, email address, or contact details.

2. Data stored on your device

Presets, custom timers, and settings (sound, vibration, keep-screen-on, digit display, sand color) are stored only on your device using local app storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Data sent by third-party SDKs

The app uses these third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — the free version shows a banner ad. AdMob may collect and share your device's advertising ID, your IP address (used for approximate location), app interactions such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Buying Pro removes ads. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it any time under Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) — anonymous usage events (app_open, timer_start with preset and duration, timer_complete, paywall_view, purchase_success) and crash reports. These include a device identifier, device model, OS version, and app version. They never include your presets or settings. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the one-time Pro purchase. Google Play holds the purchase record tied to your Google account so that Pro can be restored on reinstall. We do not receive or store your payment details. See policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications — only to alert you when a timer finishes while the screen is off. Notifications are scheduled locally; no push server is involved.
  • Vibration and keep screen on — used only while a timer is running, according to your settings.
  • Internet — to load ads and send the reports described above.

No access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Deletion

Delete local data by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of analytics, advertising, or purchase records held by the SDK providers above, email privacy@alphaloop.dev.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@alphaloop.dev
