Privacy notice for this app on Google Play

Device data

The app stores profiles, shelves, routines and locally retained photos on the device. You control the copies you export or share. Your operating system’s backup settings may apply.

Optional AI and international transfers

This privacy policy update takes effect on 2026-11-28 (Korea time). This section covers GlowLog’s optional AI on Alpha Loop Commons. In Android version code 2, AI requests covered by the existing in-app disclosure and AI sharing choice use OpenRouter.

Data and purpose. The selected ingredient-label photo and language to transcribe ingredient names. Skin profile, routines and ingredient scoring stay on-device. Photos can include identifying details visible in the image. Leave out faces and personal details.

How we route AI requests

If AI sharing is on and you request a result from a feature in this app that uses direct processing, Alpha Loop sends the inputs listed above directly to OpenAI OpCo, LLC in the United States. The first route uses OpenAI’s GPT-6 Luna through the direct API.

If the direct service is temporarily unavailable or reaches a rate or usage limit, OpenRouter handles eligible requests using GPT-6 Luna. The Worker sends photos only to GPT-6 Luna, at most once on each permitted route.

Recipients and retention

Direct OpenAI. When the request needs new processing, Alpha Loop’s Cloudflare Worker sends it over HTTPS to OpenAI to generate the requested result. OpenAI OpCo, LLC is at 1455 3rd Street, San Francisco, CA 94158, United States. The global API can process data in the United States and in other countries listed by OpenAI. privacy@openai.com · OpenAI data processing terms · Processing locations.

For direct Chat Completions, store=false turns off saving the response for later retrieval. OpenAI’s default abuse-monitoring logs can include prompts, submitted photos, responses and related metadata for up to 30 days. OpenAI may retain data longer if required by law or reasonably necessary for safety. Flagged images may be held for manual safety review. Zero Data Retention requires separate approval and configuration. API data is excluded from model training by default unless the API customer opts in. OpenAI API data controls.

Via OpenRouter. When you request a result on this route and new processing is needed, Alpha Loop sends the inputs above via HTTPS through its Cloudflare Worker to OpenRouter, Inc. (United States). To generate the result, OpenRouter forwards them to an eligible inference host for the chosen model. The host can vary by request and may process data outside Korea. support@openrouter.ai · GPT-6 Luna inference hosts · Cloudflare data processing terms.

For this route we ask OpenRouter to select endpoints eligible for zero-retention routing that disallow provider data collection. OpenRouter and the selected host apply their terms, including any applicable retention of operational metadata, or retention for legal or safety reasons. OpenRouter privacy policy · OpenRouter data processing terms.

App service. Where result replay is enabled, Alpha Loop’s service uses a request fingerprint and can provide a successful result for the same input for up to 30 days. Usage, cost and submitted reports follow the app’s own retention and deletion rules.

Your choices

In the Android versions listed above, the AI sharing choice applies to all optional AI features. Decline the in-app prompt to keep AI sharing off. To withdraw, turn off AI sharing in Settings. That stops all future AI transfers. Information already sent follows the recipient’s retention and deletion rules. You can continue keeping your skincare records. privacy@alphaloop.dev.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the account removes associated data from active app storage. Hashed receipts and associated free-use counts can remain to prevent reactivation and repeat free grants. Store and payment records follow the processors' retention policies. Remove exported copies from their storage locations.

선택한 성분표 사진·언어Worker용 동의 버전 2를 받은 뒤 HTTPS로 Cloudflare Worker에 보낸다. Worker는 위 사진 요청의 전송 조건을 적용한다.INCI 받아쓰기. 공급자 보유 기간은 운영자가 계약에서 확인한다. 중단된 요청의 사진과 요청 키는 재시도를 위해 기기에 저장한다. 설정에서 동의를 철회할 수 있다.
결과 텍스트·결과 ID·사용량사용자별 Worker 원장과 입력 캐시. 성공 입력 재생 기간 30일무료 3회, 일일 예산 및 중복 차감을 관리한다. 비대상·사용 불가 결과는 사용자 할당량을 돌려주며 공급자 사용량은 집계한다.

사용 이벤트·진단설정이 켜져 있으면 Worker에 고정 이벤트 이름, 오류 지문·코드 프레임·버전·플랫폼을 보낸다사용 통계와 안정성 개선. 설정에서 두 전송을 함께 끈다. 사진·피부 답변·자유 텍스트는 분석 속성에 포함하지 않는다.
